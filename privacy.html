Blobbiverse privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Blobbiverse is a puzzle game made by javiotic. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it. The main part covers the Android app. The other versions are covered further down.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, and javiotic doesn't collect or receive your personal data from the Android app.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to do that.
  • Google Play handles the optional purchase. javiotic never sees your payment details.

Saved on your device

The Android app stores these on your device:

  • your game progress and settings
  • when you last saw an ad, which is used to space ads out
  • whether you bought Remove ads

The app doesn't send any of this to javiotic. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device's backup, which is handled by Google under your backup settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads between levels and optional reward videos. Google says the SDK collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and encrypts it in transit:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use
  • device identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID

Google handles this data under its own policies. To learn more, read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your choice when it starts. You can change it later under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Personalized ads: you can manage them in Google's My Ad Center.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional Remove ads purchase goes through Google Play's billing system. Google handles your payment under its own terms, and the app only learns whether you own the item. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

  • Progress: use Reset progress in the game's settings.
  • Everything on your device: uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Data held by Google: use your Google account's data and privacy settings.

javiotic doesn't hold data about you from the Android app, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The Android app is intended for people aged 13 and up and isn't directed at younger children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the game, please contact us using the email below.

Other versions of the game

  • Web demo: the demo played in a browser, such as on itch.io, uses PostHog analytics to show how levels are played, for example which levels are started, finished or left and which moves fail. PostHog stores a random ID in your browser and receives your IP address and browser details. The data is sent to PostHog's EU servers. Your progress is saved in your browser.
  • Steam: the desktop game sends no analytics. It saves on your computer, and Steam achievements and cloud saves are handled by Valve under its own privacy policy.

The site you play the demo on, or buy the game from, has its own privacy policy too.

Changes to this policy
